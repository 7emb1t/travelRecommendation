About Wayfare

Wayfare is a small travel recommendation platform built on a simple idea: most people do not want a thousand search results, they want three or four places that are genuinely worth the trip. Type a keyword — a beach, a temple, a country — and we hand back a short, curated list.

What we do

Every destination in our catalogue is entered by hand, with a real photograph, a plain-language description and the current local time, so a trip stops being an abstraction before you have even booked. We do not take placement fees and we do not pad the list: if a search returns two results, it is because two results are what we would actually recommend.

How we started

Wayfare began as a front-end project and grew into something we kept using ourselves. It is built as a static site — plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework and no build step — because a travel guide should load fast on hotel wi-fi in the middle of nowhere.

Working with us

New destinations are reviewed by the editorial team before they go live. If you have a place you think belongs in the catalogue, or you want to join the team, the Contact Us page is the fastest way to reach a real person.
